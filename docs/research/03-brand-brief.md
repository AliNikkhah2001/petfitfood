# Brand brief — PetFit / پت‌فیت (asset-locked)

## Canonical assets

Saved under [`brand/`](../../brand/):

- Logo: `brand/logo/petfit-logo-catdog.png` — cat↔dog mascot + indigo wordmark
- Pack refs: `brand/packaging/petfit-label-chicken-fresh.png`, `…-fresh-beef.png`
- Live contacts on pack: **www.petfitfood.ir** · Instagram **Petfit_food**

## Positioning

Organic / human-grade fresh pet food in packages. Playful mascot, bold pack panels (orange↔blue, red↔blue by SKU).

## Locked copy

- Names: **PetFit** / **پت‌فیت**
- Tagline FA: **غذای گرم و تازه، اینبار برای کوچولوت**
- Pack line (EN/FA): Healthy food at human grade / غذای سالم در حد استاندارد انسانی
- CTA: شروع اشتراک → `/subscriptions`
- Descriptor on logo arch: ORGANIC PET FOOD PRODUCER

## Palette (from logo + chicken pack — site chrome)

Yellow/orange heavy for warmth; **indigo** from the wordmark for contrast (nav, headings, secondary panels). Green stays a thin freshness accent only (pack icons / stripe), not a brand field.

| Token | Hex | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brand-indigo` | `#30246C` | Logo wordmark / outlines |
| `brand-indigo-deep` | `#242478` | Pack navy/blue panels |
| `brand-orange` | `#F09048` | Pack primary orange |
| `brand-orange-hot` | `#F06C18` | CTA / emphasis orange |
| `brand-gold` | `#F0A830` | Logo mascot body |
| `brand-spot` | `#E87524` | Mascot spots / warm accent |
| `cream` | `#F7F4EE` | Page wash |
| `paper` | `#FFFFFF` | Cards / pack white |
| `ink` | `#2E2A20` | Body text |
| `stripe-*` | teal / magenta / lime / orange / blue | Pack footer rainbow — dividers only |
| `protein-red` | `#D8183C` | Beef SKU panel (product theming) |
| `leaf-accent` | `#7CB342` | Rare “veg / fresh” chip |

**UI rule:** Primary CTA = `brand-orange-hot`. Headers/logo text = `brand-indigo`. Hero/section washes = cream + gold. Do not make the whole site purple or green.

## Mascot (Q3 locked)

Use the official cat↔dog (and pack dachshund-twin) character — not a generic stock dog. Variants: full logo lockup · icon crop · line-art for small UI.

## Typography

- Wordmark: as in logo (rounded bold sans) — approximate with brand PNG; don’t reinvent in purple gradients.
- FA UI: Vazirmatn
- Pack headlines: bold geometric sans (Montserrat-like) for EN product names

## Motion

Unchanged: long dual bowl scenes; ingredients can echo pack line-art (chicken, carrot, potato, pumpkin).
